Create the life you want with care and support

Bass Tom turns around to face the rest of the Stage Toms. His head swivels back and forth, and they all ready themselves for the next tune, poised to hit that first note in sync. And then the song kicks in. The only one with a name I recognize. Church Camp Bride.

I can’t get to the stage, so I might as well stand here and enjoy this song. It’s the best one, by far. It just gets Tom, doesn’t it? What he was about, even before the money and the microwaves and the fame. He had Gloria and she had him and that’s all they needed. God, I wish I was up there. The chord changes are cleaner now. I know this by heart.  My hand strums against the side of my jeans before I realize it’s happening. Right after this song. I’m going up there. I’ll climb over the edge of the stage if I have to. Oh shit, here it comes. He’s gonna sing.

And then BAM. The stage lights blast us full force when the lyrics kick in. I squint my eyes and realize my ears are still in pain. Did somebody make it louder? What in the fuck? I lstumble back a step and bump an elbow. A little beer spills on my shirt. I turn to the Tom holding it and he gives me a thumbs up. It’s OK buddy, no harm done, he mouths. And then he’s WOOHOOing with all the other Toms.

I want to WOOHOO too.

Why can’t I WOOHOO?

I have to get back. I give Beer Tom a friendly tap on the arm. He winks, raises his plastic cup and lets me by. I just need less of that light, maybe it’s less intense near the back. I work my way past one Tom, and then another and another. Some nod as I pass. Some keep their eyes on the band. For whatever reason, once I’m walking towards the back, I can’t stop. I just keep on going right out the door and into the open air.
