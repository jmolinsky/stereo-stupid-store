Historical Map: TriMet Bus and MAX Routes, Portland, Oregon (early 1990s)

1990s · Bus · Light rail · Oregon · Portland · TriMet

Leave a comment

A look back at Portland's bus and MAX system in the early 1990s, when downtown's service-area icons—fish, rain, snow, beaver, leaf, rose and deer—still helped identify routes.

With routes sharing colors, the network can be difficult to follow, but it's a fascinating snapshot of Portland transit history.
