I run around to the front and there’s a small line. I try to skip it, but the door guy just points to the back of the line while he checks a middle aged woman’s ID.

When it’s finally my turn, I try to explain that I’m with the band, but the door guy just crinkles his brow and smirks.

“You trying to bring that guitar in here?”

“Yeah, I told you I’m playing,” I protest.

“The band started already and you look like everybody,” he says.

He’s gotta be mistaken and I open my case and show him the Tom Peterson sticker on my guitar. It’s the only proof I can think of.

“Ok cool, you’re in the band. That’ll be $12.50.”

This is insane. I stand there stunned for a beat, my mouth trying to make words that get me through the door to my own show, preferably without paying for the privilege.

“I’m Tom Peterson though.”

“Band goes through the back door, Tom.”

He checks another ID, stamps a wrist and a man in a suede jacket slides past me.

“The back door is locked.”

“Ok, so $12.50 then.”

I hand over a twenty and get seven back. Of course there’s no quarters.
