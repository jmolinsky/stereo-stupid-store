October 23, 2009 · Community comment

Everyone else has driven off when I put the key in the Corolla and it reluctantly comes to life. Probably the starter acting up again. I let the defrost run and hang my mask from the rearview while the windows clear. Tom smiles back at me. Off to the side, I see the window at the back of the building where this whole thing started. I was out for a walk down Ellis after a particularly shit day at work, clearing my head with a night smoke, when I passed by this place for what must have been the 900th time. Someone had sprayed the signage. I paused for one second to look at it and noticed a guy approaching the back window wearing a cardboard mask. They didn’t see me somehow and proceeded to let themselves into the old store. Then I see the flicker of a lighter go off a few times, somewhere deep inside, and then the steady glow of a dim light. Candle I’m guessing. Then, swear to god, acoustic guitar. Someone strumming softly. It made my eyes shut. When I opened them the sign read:

STEREO SUPER STUPID STORE

I took the same route the next night, but nothing. The window was unlocked when I checked. It took some effort but I opened it an inch and shut it again. Didn’t seem right to go in there alone. I kept coming back until he came back. This time there was a mask waiting for me. He put the guitar in my hand and tapped out a rhythm on the box of a long gone VCR. God, I sounded awful at first, but I took the guitar home and practiced along with YouTubes. And First Tom kept insisting. I’d try to give it back, but he waved it off and started bringing a cajon. And then it seemed like every other week or so there was somebody else joining in. On harmonica or just tapping their foot even, being part of it. We wore our Tom Peterson masks and made our music together. There was a whole case of them in the office. Nobody spoke and we just kept on not speaking.
